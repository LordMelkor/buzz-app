# Activity alerts

Get notified about more than mentions and your conversations.

## Getting started

1. To follow a channel closely, open its menu and choose **Notify for all
   activity**. Every post and reply by someone else in it then alerts.
2. To follow a person, turn on **Notify me when they post** on their profile,
   or search for them in **Settings → Activity alerts** and choose **Add**.
   Their posts in channels you're in then alert.
3. **Settings → Activity alerts** lists both, with **Remove** and **Turn off**.
   **Settings → Notifications** has a switch for each kind of alert, named
   after this card.

---

## Details

- Both lists are saved on this device. People are kept per account, and
  channels per community and account.
- The usual rules still apply: nothing while notifications are off, from your
  own posts, from messages meant only for agents, from history, from a post
  you've read, or from a muted channel. A post you're viewing stays quiet
  unless **Notify while viewing** is on.
- A post that already alerts, such as one that mentions you or replies in
  your conversation, alerts once, as before, and its own switch decides it.
  DMs and Solo chats aren't affected.
- A listed person's post in an all-activity channel alerts once, as **People
  on your list**, and that switch decides it. Their reply there alerts as
  **Channels with all activity**, as anyone's does.
- **Unfollow thread** doesn't silence replies in an all-activity channel. Mute
  the channel, or turn all activity off, to stop them.
